# 01 – Footprinting

## Overview
Footprinting is the initial information-gathering phase of cybersecurity reconnaissance. It involves collecting publicly available information about a target organization, domain, or network to understand its digital presence and potential attack surface.

## Objectives
- Identify publicly available information about the target.
- Discover domain and infrastructure details.
- Understand the target's digital footprint.
- Identify potential information exposure.

## Tools Used
- WHOIS
- DNSRecon
- TheHarvester
- WhatWeb

## Practical Activities
- Domain information gathering.
- Identification of DNS records.
- Collection of publicly available information.
- Analysis of the target's digital footprint.

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Understood the fundamentals of footprinting.
- Learned how to gather information using Kali Linux tools.
- Developed an understanding of an organization's external attack surface.
